Our Location

Our hotel is located in the heart of the Balkan Mountains, within a short walking distance from all major historical sites. Located in the middle of Bulgaria, we're a 2–3 hour drive from any major city.

How To Get There

From the Airport

Fly into Sofia Airport, then rent a car, take a train or bus, or arrange a shuttle.

From the Train Station

From Tryavna Train Station, take a taxi or arrange a shuttle.

By Car

Follow GPS navigation (Google Maps) towards str. Panorama 8, Tryavna.
